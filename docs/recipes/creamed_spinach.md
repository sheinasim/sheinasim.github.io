# Creamed Spinach 

**Description:** Creamed spinach can be made with less dairy if you replace the heavy cream and creamed cheese with vegan options. 

**Yields:** About 8 servings of laksa inspired stew 
**Prep time:** 30 minutes
**Cook time:** 30 minutes

---

## Ingredients

- 2 Tbl butter 
- 1 shallot finely minced
- 2 cloves garlic finely grated
- 3/4 cup heavy cream (can substitute with vegan option) 
- 1/2 cup cream cheese (can subtitute with vegan option)
- 20 oz frozen spinach, thawed, cooked, and squeezed
- 1 tea chicken bouillon 
- 1 cup parmesan 
- nutmeg

---

## Instructions

1. Heat butter in a 3-5 quart (liter) pot 
2. Saute shallots until soft, add garlic, saute until fragrant.
3. Slowly add heavy cream, melt in cream cheese.
4. Stir in cooked and squeezed spinach.
5. Melt in parmesan, stir in chicken bouillon.
6. Add grated nutmeg, salt, and pepper to taste.

---

## Notes 

*   Serve warm as a side to juicy steak.
