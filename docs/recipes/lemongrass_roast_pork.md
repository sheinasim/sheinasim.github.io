# Lemongrass roast pork 

**Description:** Roast pork for sandwiches or soups 

**Yields:** About 1.5 lbs of roast pork
**Prep time:** 45 minutes
**Cook time:** Overnight marinade and 3 hours in the oven

---

## Ingredients

### Marinade

- 3 lbs pork shoulder
- 6 cloves minced garlic
- 4 stalks lemongrass minced
- 4 Tbls fish sauce
- 2 Tbls vegetable oil 
- 4 Tbls dark brown sugar
- 2 Tbls corn starch
- 1 tea ground black pepper

### Before roasting

- Vegetable oil
- Ground black pepper

---

## Instructions

1. Combine marinade ingredients in a vacuum bag and add pork.  
2. Cover pork and vacuum seal bag. 
3. Marinate overnight in the refrigerator. 
4. Pre-heat oven to 450F. 
5. Extract pork from the marinade and remove large marinade pieces like lemon grass and garlic to prevent burning. Tie with kitchen twineto form compact roast and pat dry. 
6. Coat with vegetable oil and then ground black pepper
7. Insert meat thermometer, and roast at 450F for 20 minutes, then lower to 325F for 3 hours or until internal temperature registers 205F.
8. Remove from heat and allow to rest for at least 15 minutes before slicing. 

---

## Notes 

*   Serve in a bahn mi or over noodles.
