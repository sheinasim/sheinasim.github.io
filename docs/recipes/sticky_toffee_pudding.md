# Sticky toffee pudding  

**Description:** Inspired by NYT recipe. 

**Yields:** 9x13" tray of pudding and sauce
**Prep time:** 60 minutes
**Cook time:** 30 minutes + 1 minute under broiler

---

## Ingredients

### Pudding
- 1 cup of chopped dates
- 1 cup boiling water
- 3 Tbl cold butter cubed
- 1 tea baking soda
- 1/2 tea salt
- 80 g Demarara sugar (sugar in the raw)
- 80 g brown sugar
- 2 eggs
- 130 g AP flour
- 5 g vanilla extract

### Topping
- 75 g butter (5 Tbl) 
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 75 g brown sugar
- 1/2 tea salt

### Extra sauce
- 45 g butter (3 Tbl)
- 1 1/4 cup heavy cream
- 40 g brown sugar
- 1/2 tea salt

---

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350 F. 
2. Prepare 9x13" pan with butter.
3. Soak dates in boiling water for at least 5 minutes.
4. Add everything but the dates in a food processor and pulse until combined.
5. Add dates and 1/2 cup of date water and pulse until smooth and some date specks remain.
6. Pour batter into pan and bake for 30 minutes (until set).
7. While pudding is baking, make sauce.
8. Heat sugar in sauce pan until liquid. Add butter and mix until homogenous.
9. While still on the heat, add cream and salt and whisk. 
10. Once pudding is set, remove from oven and poke holes on the top. Pour sauce onto pudding and broil for 1 minute 5 inches from the broiler. Watch closely so it does not burn.
11. While pudding is cooling, make extra sauce using the same steps as above.

---

## Notes 

* Same recipe as NYT, but instructions differ.
