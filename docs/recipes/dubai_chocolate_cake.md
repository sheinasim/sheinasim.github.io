# Dubai Chocolate Cake 

**Description:** Cake inspired by Dubai chocolate. Original came from Platin' it with Wendy (recipe no longer available online) 

**Yields:** A three layer 7" inch cake
**Prep time:** 3 hours
**Cook time:** 1 hour

---

## Ingredients


### Chocolate cake
- 400 g all-purpose flour
- 125 g cocoa powder
- 1 Tbl baking soda
- 1 1/2 tea baking powder
- 1 1/2 tea salt
- 500 g granulated sugar
- 4 large eggs
- 355 mL buttermilk
- 125 mL vegetable oil
- 1 shot espresso + hot water to make 1 1/2 cups OR 1 Tbl instant coffee powder and 1 1/2 cup hot water OR 1/2 cup cold brew concentrate and 1 cup hot water
- 2 tea vanilla extract

### Chocolate ganache
- 650 g chocolate chips
- 400 mL heavy cream (can be vegan)
- 15 g butter

###  Kataifi pistachio filling
- 250 g kataifi
- 50 g butter
- 225 g pistachio cream
- 1/2 tea salt

### Simple syrum
- 3 Tbl sugar in 120 mL water

---

## Instructions

### Chocolate cake
1. Preheat oven to 350 F. 
2. Prepare 7" cake pans by lining with butter and dusting the sides with cocoa powder, and lining the bottom with parchment. 
3. Sift dry cake ingredients together and mix in sugar.
4. Wet ingredients and mix until combined and smooth.
5. Pour into prepared pans and bake 30 min or until toothpick comes out clean, but the cake is still making light hissing/bubbling sounds.
6. Cool in the cake pns on wire racks.

### Chocolate ganache
1. Simmer cream on medium low and pour over chocolate in a bowl with butter. 
2. Allow to sit for 5 minutes and mix util combined and smooth. 
3. Pour into a disposable pastry bag and refrigerate until ready to assemble cake. 

### Kataifi filling
1. Melt butter and add to kataifi.
2. Add pistachio cream and salt, mix until combined.

### Cake assembly
1. Prepare the simple sugar syrup.
2. Level cakes and place one layer on your serving plate with a little ganache underneath so it doesn't slide around.
3. Spoon 1/3 of the simple syrup on the cake and then add a thin layer of ganache.
4. Pipe the ganache around top of the cake to form a border. Fill top with half of the kataifi filling.
5. Repeat with the next layer. 
6. Put the final layer on top, cover with ganache, and add the remaining kataifi filling decoratively on top.
7. Cover the sides evenly with ganache or leave minimally naked.
