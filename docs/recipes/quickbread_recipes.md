# Sourdough Quick Breads 

**Description:** Sourdough quick breads with different flavors 

**Yields:** 1 loaf 
**Prep time:** 30 minutes
**Cook time:** 60 minutes

---

---

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350 F. 
2. Sift together dry
3. Combine wet
4. Add dry to wet
5. Prepare pan (butter and line with parchment)
6. Pour batter into prepared pan
7. Bake until internal temperature of 190F (50min)
8. Allow to cool for 1 1/2 hour. Serve at room temperature or chilled.
