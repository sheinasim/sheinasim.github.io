# Salmon Chowder 

**Description:** Almost Pike Place Salmon Chowder 

**Yields:** About 16 servings of salmon chowder
**Prep time:** 30 minutes
**Cook time:** 30 minutes

---

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup (1 stick) butter
- 2 medium onions diced
- 8 stalks celery diced
- 1 lb carrots diced
- 2/3 cups (80g) flour
- 8 cups lobster bouillon
- 6 small potatoes cubed
- 8 oz cream cheese
- 2 cups heavy cream
- 12 oz smoked salmon
- 1/4 cup capers
- garlic salt to taste
- white papper to taste

---

## Instructions

1. Melt butter in large pot. 
2. Saute onions. 
3. Once onions are soft, add diced celery and carrots. 
4. Once fragrant, add flour, mix, and toast to make a roux.
5. Add lobster bouillon a little at a time, mix, until absorbed by roux. 
6. Repeat with remainder of the roux, a little at a time until all bouillon is mixed in and no lumps remain. 
7. Add potatoes, heat until fork tender.
8. Melt in cream cheese and then add heavy cream.
9. Once heated through add capers and cooked smoked salmon.

---

## Notes 

*   Serve warm with crusty bread.
