# French Onion Soup 

**Description:** Inspired by Panera Bread's French onion soup.  

**Yields:** About 16 servings 
**Prep time:** 30 minutes
**Cook time:** 60 minutes

---

## Ingredients

- 1/4 cup (4 Tbl, or half a stick) of butter
- 8 cups (4-5 medium) yellow onions sliced pole to pole (vertically)
- 3 Tbl (24 g) AP flour
- 28 oz of beef broth
- 3/4 cup chicken broth
- 1 1/2 cup water
- 1 Tbl ketchup
- 1 1/2 tea black pepper
- 1 1/2 tea salt
- 1/2 tea (4 sprigs) fresh thyme
- 1/2 tea garlic powder

---

## Instructions

1. Melt butter, then saute onions for 15-20 minutes on medium heat. 
2. Add flour, mix through until flour is slightly toasted.
3. Slowly add liquids, incorporating slowly to prevent lumps.
4. Once all liquids are combined, add remaining ingredients.
5. Bring up to boil, then reduce heat to simmer and heat uncovered for 20 min.

---

## Notes 

*   Serve with warm crusty and buttered bread.
