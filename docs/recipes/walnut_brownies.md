# Walnut brownies 

**Description:** Gooey walnut brownies 

**Yields:** About 1 lb of candied nuts
**Prep time:** 30 minutes
**Cook time:** 30-35 minutes

---

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup cocoa 
- 1/2 cup (1 stick/8 Tbl/115 g) butter
- 2 eggs
- 1 cup (200 g) sugar
- 1/4 cup (30 g) AP flour
- 1 cup chopped walnuts
- 1 tea vanilla
- 1/2 tea salt

---

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 325 F. 
2. Melt butter in a sauce pan and combine with cocoa.
3. Take off the heat and allow to cool, combine with eggs and vanilla.
4. Combine dry ingredients add to wet.
5. Pour into greased 8x8 pan, bake for 30-35 min.

---

## Notes 

* Serve warm with vanilla ice cream.
