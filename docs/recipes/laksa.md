# Sheina's "Laksa" 

**Description:** No laksa paste, no problem, this "laksa" doesn't laksa in flavor. 

**Yields:** About 16 servings of laksa inspired stew 
**Prep time:** 30 minutes
**Cook time:** 30 minutes

---

## Ingredients

- 2 Tbl Neutral oil
- 2 shallots finely minced
- 2 inches (5 cm) of ginger finely grated
- 4 cloves garlic finely grated
- 250 g red Thai curry paste 
- 25 g green Thai curry paste
- 1200 ml combined coconut milk and chicken broth
- fish sauce to taste

## Extra sauce

- 1/4 cup sambal olek
- 1 Tbl brown or palm sugar (to taste)
- 1 Tbl fish sauce
- 1 Tbl lime juice

---

## Instructions

1. Heat oil in a 3-5 quart (liter) pot 
2. Saute shallots until soft, add ginger and garlic, saute until fragrant.
3. Add curry pastes and saute until slightly darkened.
4. Slowly add coconut milk and chicken broth.
5. Heat through until flavors meld.  

---

## Notes 

*   Serve warm with squeeze of fresh lime, and noodles and proteins of choice (fried tofu, shrimp, etc).
