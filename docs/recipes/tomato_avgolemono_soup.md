# Tomato avgolemono soup 

**Description:** Variation on Serious Eats recipe. 

**Yields:** About 16 servings  
**Prep time:** 30 minutes
**Cook time:** 60 minutes

---

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup (8 Tbl, one stick) of butter or oil
- 12 scallions or equivalent chopped
- 1 bunch celery (8 or so ribs) chopped
- 8 cups stock (veggie or chicken)
- 2 cups water
- 1 cup white wine
- 2 28 oz canned tomatoes
- 1 tea cinnamon
- 6 bay leaves
- 1 cup Basmati rice
- 1 bunch parsley chopped
- 4 egg, yolks and whites separate
- 5 Tbl lemon juice

---

## Instructions

1. Heat oil in a large pot. 
2. Saute onions and celery until fragrant (~8 min).
3. Add cinnamon, toast, and deglze with white wine. 
4. Add stock, water, tomatoes, and bay leaves.
5. Bring to boil, reduce heat, cover, and simmer for 45 min.
6. Add rice and cook until soft. Add parsley.
7. In a separate bowl, add egg yolks and whisk with lemon juice.
8. Temper egg yolks with hot soup.
9. Whisk in egg whites into pot.
10. Turn off heat and slowly mix yolks into soup.

---

## Notes 

*  Serve with warm, crusty, and buttered bread. Fall in love again.
