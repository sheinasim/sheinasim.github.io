# Pecan bars 

**Description:** Gooey pecan bars like pecan pie but without the excess goo. 

**Yields:** 9x13" tray of pecan bars 
**Prep time:** 30 minutes
**Cook time:** 25 minutes

---

## Ingredients

### Crust
- 230 g AP flour
- 75g sugar
- 1/2 tea salt
- 8 Tbl (115 g) butter

### Filling
- 4 cups of pecans (14 oz to 1 lb) chopped 
- 8 Tbl (115 g) butter
- 1 tea vanilla 
- 1 1/2 tea bourbon
- 156 g corn syrup
- 150 g brown sugar
- 1/2 tea salt

---

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350 F. 
2. Line 9x13" pan with parchment paper.
3. In a rimmed baking sheet, toast pecans for 5 minutes. Alternatively, heat for 30 seconds to 1 minute in the microwave. 
4. In a medium bowl, combine dry ingredients of crust then mix with butter until homogenous. 
5. Press crust into 9x13" pan.
6. Without the pecans, combine filling ingredients together. Once homogenous, mix in pecans. 
7. Pour filling over the crust. 
8. Bake for 20-25 min until pecans start to darken and crust is set. 
9. Allow to cool for 1 1/2 hour. Serve at room temperature or chilled.

---

## Notes 

*  Pecan pie without the excess goo.
