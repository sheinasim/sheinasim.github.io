# King cake for Mardi Gras

**Description:** King cake to celebrate Mardi Gras. Add plastic baby at your own risk. 

**Yields:** 2 cakes 
**Prep time:** 1 hour active, 2 hours passive
**Cook time:** 25 minutes

---

## Ingredients

### Brioche

- 750 g AP flour
- 1 cup whole milk
- 1/4 cup (half a stick) butter
- 2/3 cup 110F/45C water
- 4 1/2 tea active dry yeast
- 100 g granulated sugar (divided, 10 g and 90 g)
- 2 large eggs
- 1 1/2 tea salt
- 1/2 grated nutmeg

### Filling

- 200 g brown sugar
- 3/4 cup chopped pecans
- 60 g AP flour
- 1 Tbl cinnamon
- 1/2 cup (one stick) butter
- 8 oz (one brick) cream cheese, kept separated

### Cream cheese glaze

- 6 Tbl (3/4 stick) butter
- 4 oz (1/2 brick) cream cheese
- 2 cups powdered sugar
- 2 1/2 tea lemon juice
- 1/4 tea salt
- Purple, green, and gold sanding sugar

---

## Instructions

1. Melt butter in milk in a pot or in the microwave and allow to cool 10 minutes. 
2. Combine yeast, water, and 10 g of sugar and let sit for 10 minutes.
3. Combine milk mixture, eggs, salt, nutmeg, remaining 90 g of sugar, and yeast mixture in a stand mixer bowl. 
4. Using the dough hook, mix in 750 g of AP flour one cup at a time. Mix until combined, about 3 minutes.
5. Transfer to lightly floured surface and knead by hand until smooth, about 10 minutes. 
6. Transfer dough back to mixing bowl and cover. Allow to rise until doubled about 1 to 1 1/2 hours depending on the temperature of your kitchen.
7. While dough is proofing, combine filling all ingredients except cream cheese. Mix until homogenous.
8. Once proofed, split dough into two, and roll one into a 10" x 16" rectangle. 
9. Evenly spread half the filling ingredients onto the rectanglular dough. 
10. Cut half a brick of cream cheese into 32 pieces and spread evenly onto the rectangular dough.
11. Roll starting on a long edge to make one long tube and form into a ring by joining the ends. Make large slashes along the ring like large ticks on a clock.
12. Repeat with the second half of the dough.
13. Allow to rise another 30 to 45 min and preheat oven to 375 F or 190 C.
14. Bake 12 minutes and rotate and bake another 13 minutes or until internal temperature reads 180 F
15. Remove from oven and allow to cool completely. 
16. Combine ingredients for cream cheese glaze except for the sanding sugar.
17. Glaze completely cooked cakes evenly. Decorate with stripes of sanding sugar, alternating colors purple, green, and gold (about three stripes of each). The stripes should go around the ring forming lines from the inside to the outside of the ring. 
18. Put a baby somewhere in it for tradition, or don't if you don't want to.

---

## Notes 

*  Serve the following day with coffee or tea.
