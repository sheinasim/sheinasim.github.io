# Carne asada marinade 

**Description:** Carne asada marinade inspired by Serious Eats 

**Yields:** About 1.5 cups of carne asada marinade
**Prep time:** 30 minutes
**Marinade time:** 12 hours
**Cook time:** ~60 minutes

---

## Ingredients

- 6 dried chiles, some combination of pasilla, guajillo, ancho, and California stems and seeds removed 
- 6 cloves garlic
- 180 mL fresh orange juice (can add calamansi juice to achieve volume)
- 30 mL fresh lime juice
- 2 chipotles in adobo
- 30 mL olive oil
- 30 mL soy sauce
- 30 mL fish sauce
- 1 bunch cilantro
- 1 Tbl cumin seeds
- 1 tea coriander seeds
- 2 tea salt

---

## Instructions

1. Toast chiles on a plate in the microwave for 20 seconds
2. Combine all ingredients in a blender and blend until smooth
3. Marinate 1-2 lbs of beef of your choosing for 12 + hours
4. Grill to medium rare or as appropriate for your cut of meat
5. Slice meat thinly or chop into small pieces 

---

## Notes 

*   Serve over tortillas, with salsa, avocado, and other taco toppings
