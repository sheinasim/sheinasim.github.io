# Pizza dough recipe	 

**Description:** Cold fermented pizza dough 

**Yields:** 3 16" pizzas 
**Prep time:** 30 minutes

---

## Ingredients

- 535 g AP flour 
- 2 g instant dry yeast
- 360 g 40F water
- 12 g salt 
- Olive oil for coating bowls 

---

## Instructions

1. In a stand mixer bowl, combine dry ingredients, and then wet, mix until combined, and kneax with the dough hook for 15 minutes. 
2. Separate into 3 303 g balls into oiled 1 liter bowls. 
3. Refrigerate overnight and roll into pizza the next day. 
4. Before use, warm up with the oven light for about 30 min.

---

## Notes 

* Stretch by hand
