# Hot fudge sauce 

**Description:** Hot fudge sauce for ice cream 

**Yields:** About 2 cups of suace
**Prep time:** 15 minutes
**Cook time:** 30 minutes

---

## Ingredients

- 2/3 cup heavy whipping cream 
- 1/2 cup light corn syrup
- 66g brown sugar
- 1/4 cup cocoa powder
- 1/2 tea salt
- 6 oz milk chocolate
- 2 Tbl butter
- 1 tea vanilla

---

## Instructions

1. Combine cream, corn syrup, sugar, cocoa, salt, and half the chocolate in a sauce pan.
2. Stir over moderate heat until chocolate is melted.
3. Lower heat to low and heavy simmer/low boil for 5 minutes.
4. Remove from heat, add butter, vanilla, and remaining chocolate.
5. Cool to warm.
6. Serve warm.

---

## Notes 

* Serve with ice cream or brownies or eat by the spoonful.
