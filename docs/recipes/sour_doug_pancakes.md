# Sour Doug Pancakes 

**Description:** Sourdough pancakes made from our starter named Sir Doug H. Loafman 

**Yields:** About 6 9" pancakes
**Prep time:** 15 minutes
**Cook time:** 15 minutes

---

## Ingredients

- ~300 grams sourdough discard
- 2 Tbls granulated sugar
- 2 eggs
- 4 Tbl olive oil
- 1/2 tea salt
- 1/4 tea baking soda in 1 Tbl water
- Butter for greasing the pan

---

## Instructions

1. Preheat pan to medium heat. 
2. Combine all ingredients except baking soda in water.
3. Once pan is heated, grease with pat of butter. 
4. Add baking soda to batter and mix. 
5. Use large ice cream scoop to scoop batter to pan. 
6. Cook pancake until bubbles set, then flip. 
7. Cook second side another ~30 seconds more. 
8. Repeat with rest of the batter, keep finished pancakes in the oven with the light on. 

---

## Notes 

*   Serve with butter, syrup, bananas, blueberries, and anything else you want to eat with pancakes.
