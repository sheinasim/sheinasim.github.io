# Basque cheesecake (naturally gluten free) 

**Description:** Crustless cheesecake with burnt top. 

**Yields:** 9 inch cheesecake
**Prep time:** 30 minutes
**Cook time:** 50 minutes

---

## Ingredients

- 2 lb (32 oz or 4 bricks) room temperature full fat cream cheese (can warm up under oven light to hurry warming)
- 7 eggs
- 3/4 cup heavy cream
- 2 tea vanilla 
- 350 g granulated sugar 
- 3/4 tea sea salt  

---

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 400 F. 
2. Line 9" spring form pan completely with parchment 
3. In a stand mixer bowl, cream together all the cream cheese with sugar until homogenous.
4. Beat eggs in a bowl and slowly add to cream cheese.
5. Add remaining ingredients and mix until homogenous. 
6. Pass mixture through a sieve and then pour into lined spring form pan. 
7. Tap filled pan on the counter to release bubbles in the batter. 
8. Bake for 47-50 minutes until instant read thermometer reads 155-165 F. Cheesecake should be dark brown on top, but still jiggly in the middle. 
9. Allow to cool for 2 hours and then refrigerate overnight.

---

## Notes 

* Naturally gluten free. 
* Flavor with ube by adding 150g ube halaya and replace the vanilla with 2 teaspoons ube extract added at the same time as the heavy whipping cream.
