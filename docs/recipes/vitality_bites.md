# Kruger National Park Vitality Bites 

**Description:** Date squares with cookies and fruit 

**Yields:** 200 g brick 
**Prep time:** 15 minutes
**Cool time:** 4 hours

---

## Ingredients

- 90 g pitted dates 
- 32 g butter
- 34 g Marie biscuits 
- 22 g dried fruit (cherries or pineapple) 
- 1/2 tea salt
- 11 g granulated sugar 

---

## Instructions

1. In a food processor, blend together everything but the biscuits and dried fruit until homogenous. 
2. On a sheet 12" x 8" sheet of parchment paper, pile half of the processed dates, then half the biscuits, the remaining dates, and the remaining biscuits.
3. Fold the excess parchment on top of the pile and compress dates and biscuits, breaking the biscuits into smaller pieces about 1/8 the original size. 
4. Wrap the mixture into a dense and evenly shaped brick. 
5. Refrigerate for 4 hours or overnight.  
6. Cut and serve. 

---

## Notes 

* Delicious snack popular at Kruger National Park in South Africa.
