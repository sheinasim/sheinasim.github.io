# Candied Nuts 

**Description:** Candied nuts to top salads or a delicious snack. 

**Yields:** About 1 lb of candied nuts
**Prep time:** 15 minutes
**Cook time:** 60 minutes

---

## Ingredients

- 1 lb (16 oz) walnuts (or pecans) 
- 1 egg white
- 1 tea vanilla 
- 150 g granulated sugar 
- 2 tea sea salt 
- 2 tea cinnamon 

---

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 250 F. 
2. Grease a rimmed baking sheet with butter (do not use foil, do not use parchment).
3. Combine sugar, salt, and cinnamon in a small bowl. 
4. In a large bowl, beat egg white to stiff peaks. 
5. Add vanilla and wallnuts to beaten egg whites, mix until evenly coated. 
6. Add sugar mixture and mix until nuts are evenly coated. 
7. Spread nuts into an even layer on the baking sheet. 
8. Bake for 60 minutes, turning them every 15 minutes. Bake for entire 60 minutes even if they look finished at 45.
9. Allow to cool and serve, freeze, or refrigerate.

---

## Notes 

*   Serve with salad or snack on alone.
